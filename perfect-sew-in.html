The ‘Perfect’ Sew In

£130 | 5 hrs

Lasts 4 to 6 weeks (up to 8 weeks max)

Includes:

  • Flat, long lasting braid down
  • No leave out, so your natural hair is fully protected
  • Bring your own bundles: 3 is standard, more if you prefer a fuller look
  • Styling included: layers, curls, crimp or straighten
  • 2 part sew in or flip over add‑on available
  • A small amount of hair from your bundles will need to be cut for the parting
  • Easy removal

Near South East London and need a no leave out sew in? Visit the Les Braid It studio near Old Kent Road. The ‘Perfect’ Sew In is a no leave-out, no-lace, no-glue sew in inspired by the magic part sew in, made for a realistic ‘natural’ root look. This method is meant to give the illusion of a leave out. It works best on textured hair, though all textures are welcome.

Before your appointment

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

Aftercare

  • Tie a scarf right after your appointment, and every night after that.
  • Cleanse your scalp at least every 2 weeks, and dry the braids underneath fully to keep your hair and scalp healthy.
  • Brush the bundles gently from the ends upwards.
  • Keep heat low on any synthetic hair used.

FAQs

How long does The 'Perfect' Sew In last?

4 to 6 weeks, and up to 8 weeks with good aftercare.

What is a no leave out sew in?

None of your own hair is left out. The parting is created so it looks like it grows from your scalp.

How many bundles do I need?

3 bundles are standard. Bring more if you prefer a fuller look.

Does it work on my hair type?

It works best on textured hair, but all textures are welcome.

How do I take it out?

You will be given takedown instructions after your appointment. The ‘Perfect’ Sew In Takedown service is coming soon.
