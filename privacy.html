Privacy Policy

This explains what personal details Les Braid It collects, why, and what you can ask me to do with them. Last updated 27 October 2026.

Who I am

  • Les Braid It is run by Lilian, a hairstylist in South East London. I am responsible for your personal details.
  • You can reach me at info@lesbraidit.co.uk or on WhatsApp on +44 7349 760911.

What I collect

  • When you book: your name, email address, phone number, the style, add-ons, date and time, and whether you agree to photos or videos of your hair being shared.
  • When you pay a deposit: whether the payment went through. Card payments are handled by Stripe, so I never see or store your card details.
  • Custom style requests: the photo you upload, your description, and any messages between us.
  • Member accounts: your login email, name, phone number, your bookings and your loyalty card progress.
  • Enquiry forms: the details you type in, such as for the classes or collaborations.
  • If you booked through my old website, your past booking details were moved here so your history is kept.

Why I use it

  • To book, confirm, remind you about and manage your appointments, and to contact you about them. This is needed to provide the service you asked for.
  • To keep records of payments for my accounts and tax, which the law requires.
  • To share photos or videos of your hair, but only if you said yes on your booking form. You can change your mind at any time.
  • I do not sell your details or use them for advertising.

Who helps me store and send it

  • Supabase: stores bookings, accounts and custom requests securely.
  • Stripe: takes deposit payments.
  • Brevo: sends booking confirmation and update emails.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts: provides the fonts on this website, so your browser connects to Google to load them.
  • These companies only use your details to provide their service to me.

How long I keep it

  • Booking and payment records are kept for up to 6 years, because accounts and tax records must be kept for that long.
  • Custom request photos and messages are kept only as long as needed for your request, and you can ask me to delete them.
  • Member accounts are kept until you ask me to close them.

Your rights

  • You can ask to see the details I hold about you, to correct them, or to have them deleted (unless I must keep them for tax records).
  • You can ask me to stop using your details for something, such as sharing photos of your hair.
  • To ask, contact me by email or WhatsApp. I will reply within one month.
  • If you are unhappy with how I handle your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and browser storage

  • If you log in, your browser stores a small login key so you stay signed in, and a note of whether you are logged in so the menu shows the right links.
  • This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
