Shuku and base braids in South East London, with hair included, at the Les Braid It studio near Old Kent Road. Patewo/Shuku and Base is a classic, elegant style combining a fan-shaped patewo or ponytail shuku with a ‘base’ (a fringe).

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

  • Use a leave-on serum to keep your scalp healthy.

FAQs

How long does Patewo/Shuku and Base last?

3 to 4 weeks, and up to 6 weeks with good aftercare.

What is Shuku?

A traditional Yoruba style where braids go up towards the crown and gather in a bun or point.

How is this different from the £30 Shuku/Patewo?

This one uses braiding hair for the bun. The £30 one is done on your natural hair.
