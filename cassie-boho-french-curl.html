Before your appointment

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

Aftercare

  • Keep the curls hydrated with water and an oil.
  • Use a Tangle Teezer while the hair is wet, always working from the bottom up.
  • Put your hair in a loose pineapple under a satin bonnet at night.
  • Trim curls that tangle rather than pulling them apart.
  • Keep the curls light: a water and leave-in spray every few days is enough.

FAQs

How long does 'Cassie' Small Knotless Boho French Curl last?

4 to 6 weeks, and up to 8 weeks with good aftercare.

What is the difference between French curls and boho braids?

French curls: the hair is braided down part of the length (usually as box or knotless braids), then turns into loose, bouncy, spiral curls at the ends instead of being braided all the way to the tips.

Boho braids: box or knotless braids where loose wavy or curly strands are left out or fed in all the way down the length of the braid, not just at the bottom.
