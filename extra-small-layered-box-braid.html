Before your appointment

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

Aftercare

  • Sleep in a satin bonnet or on a satin pillowcase to keep frizz down.
  • Keep your scalp hydrated with a hydrating leave-on serum.
  • Wash your scalp gently every 2 to 3 weeks and dry the braids fully afterwards.
  • Avoid tight, heavy updos in the first few days.
  • Be gentle with takedown, and make sure your hair is moisturised.

FAQs

How long does Extra Small Layered Box Braid last?

4 to 6 weeks, and up to 8 weeks with good aftercare.

How long do extra small box braids take?

About 7.5 hours, plus 1.5 hours for waist or hip length.

Are box braids or knotless braids better for my hair?

Knotless braids start with your own hair, so there is less tension at the root. Box braids start with braiding hair from the root. Ultimately, it is the stylist’s job to control tension.
