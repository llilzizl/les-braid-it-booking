The ‘Perfect’ U Part Install

£90 | 4 hrs

Lasts 4 to 6 weeks (up to 8 weeks max)

Includes:

  • All you need to bring is your U part wig and an extra bundle of hair for the parting
  • Flat, long lasting braid down
  • No leave out, so your natural hair is fully protected
  • Styling included: layers, curls, crimp or straighten

A no leave out U part install in South East London, at the Les Braid It studio near Old Kent Road. The 'Perfect' U Part Install is a no leave-out, no-lace, no-glue method inspired by the magic part technique, giving a realistic natural root look. A quick, low-fuss way to switch up your style in London.

Before your appointment

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

Aftercare

  • Wrap or bonnet your hair every night.
  • Cleanse your scalp through the parts every 1 to 2 weeks, and dry the braids underneath fully.
  • Brush the bundles gently from the ends upwards.
  • Keep heat low on any leave out or blended part.
  • Keep the parting clean and dry, and avoid heavy oils at the part so it stays looking like your scalp.

FAQs

How long does The 'Perfect' U Part Install last?

4 to 6 weeks, and up to 8 weeks with good aftercare.

What is the difference between a U part and a sew in?

The U part creates one open parting area. A full sew in has the parting built into the sewn bundles.

Do I need leave out?

No. This method needs no leave out.

What do I need to bring?

Your U part wig and an extra bundle of hair for the parting.
