Every style at Les Braid It is finished with YMB London Braiding Gel, an amazing gel made with natural ingredients.

Why a natural braiding gel?

Braiding gel sits on your scalp and edges for weeks while you wear a protective style, so what is in it really matters. Many conventional gels use drying alcohols, silicones and harsh preservatives that can leave flakes, build up and a dry, itchy scalp. Choosing a natural, plant based gel means your braids stay sleek while your hair and scalp stay moisturised, which helps protect your edges and keeps your natural hair healthy under your style.

About the gel

Achieve sleek, long-lasting braids and styles with our Vegan Braiding Gel, the ultimate styling solution designed for all hair types. Crafted with plant-based, cruelty-free ingredients, this gel provides exceptional hold without the flaking, stiffness, or stickiness found in conventional products.

Infused with nourishing oils like coconut and jojoba, as well as hydrating aloe vera, it promotes scalp health and keeps hair moisturised while taming frizz and flyaways. Whether you're creating intricate cornrows, box braids, twists, or protective styles, our vegan gel offers a smooth application and a lightweight finish that lasts all day.

Free from parabens, sulfates, silicones, and artificial fragrances, this eco-friendly formula ensures your hair stays healthy and your conscience stays clear. Elevate your braiding game with a gel that's kind to your hair, your scalp, and the planet!

3 ingredients your scalp will love

  • Aloe leaf juice

    The first ingredient in the gel. Aloe vera hydrates and soothes the scalp, helping to calm itchiness and dryness while your braids are in.

  • Castor seed oil

    A rich oil that seals moisture into the hair and softens the edges, helping to reduce dryness and breakage around the hairline.

  • Avocado oil

    Full of vitamins and healthy fats that nourish the hair, add softness and shine, and help keep strands strong under tension.
