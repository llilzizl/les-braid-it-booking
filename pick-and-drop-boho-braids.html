Boho braids in South East London, with braiding hair included, at the Les Braid It studio near Old Kent Road. Straight Pick & Drop Boho Braids give a relaxed, undone curl finish that's full of texture and movement. A boho protective style that's as easy to wear as it is beautiful.

Before your appointment

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

Aftercare

  • Keep the curls hydrated with a water and leave-in spray, then scrunch.
  • Finger-detangle the curls, never brush them.
  • Put your hair in a loose pineapple under a satin bonnet at night.
  • Trim curls that tangle rather than pulling them apart.
  • The picked and dropped strands are straight, so smooth them with a little mousse rather than water to keep them sleek.

FAQs

How long do Straight Pick & Drop Boho Braids last?

4 to 6 weeks, and up to 8 weeks with good aftercare.

What are pick and drop braids?

Braids where loose hair is left out along the length, so they look fuller and softer.

How do I stop boho strands tangling?

Keep them hydrated, finger detangle and wear a bonnet at night.
