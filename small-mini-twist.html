Small Mini Twist / Mini Braids

£40 | 2 hrs

Lasts up to 4 weeks

Includes:

  • Small twists or braids on your own hair (7 rows from the middle part to the ear)
  • Light, low tension protective style
  • Add Marley hair for extra thickness, or 1 to 2 inches of length
  • No gel option available

Reference photos can be added to all bookings.

Mini twists on your natural hair in South East London, at the Les Braid It studio near Old Kent Road.

Before your appointment

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

Aftercare

  • Keep the style moisturised with a light leave-in every few days.
  • Wear a bonnet at night to stop frizz and fuzzing.
  • Take the style down gently and detangle in sections.

FAQs

How long do Small Mini Twist / Mini Braids last?

Up to 4 weeks with good aftercare.

Are mini twists good for hair growth?

They keep your ends tucked away and need little daily styling, which helps protect length.
