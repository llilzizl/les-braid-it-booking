Micro braids with a sew in, in South East London, at the Les Braid It studio near Old Kent Road. Micro Braids with Sew In pairs micro braids with weave for the look without the time.

Before your appointment

Please read the Hair Prep guide so your hair is properly washed, detangled and ready. It helps your style last longer and keeps your hair healthy.

Aftercare

  • Sleep in a satin bonnet or on a satin pillowcase to keep frizz down.
  • Keep your hair in twists or braids overnight and during exercise.
  • Brush your hair regularly and thoroughly.
  • Wash your scalp gently every 2 to 3 weeks and dry the braids fully afterwards.
  • Avoid tight, heavy updos in the first few days.
  • Treat the braids like knotless braids and the sewn section like a sew in: dry both fully after washing.

FAQs

How long do Micro Braids with Sew In last?

4 to 6 weeks, and up to 8 weeks with good aftercare.

Why mix micro braids and a sew in?

You get the look of very fine braids with the volume of a sew in, in less time than full micro braids.

Is the sew in hair included?

No. The braiding hair is included, but bring 3 bundles for the sew in, or more if you prefer.
